Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest places to keep supplied with hot water. Every sink, sprayer, and dish cycle draws from the tank, a lunch rush can empty a small heater in minutes, and a high-temperature dish machine needs a booster to reach its sanitizing temperature. Paterson Water Heater sizes, repairs, and maintains the tanks, tankless units, and booster heaters that keep the kitchen and restrooms supplied, and we schedule service around your hours.

Hot water temperature is a core issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature can stop service. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and flow your operation depends on, with documented service records that show a well-maintained kitchen to health inspectors and landlords. You get detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Hotels & Multifamily

Hot Water for Hotels, Apartments, and Large Facilities

Scale changes everything. One failed tank in a hotel can leave an entire floor with lukewarm showers, and a multifamily building with a single central heater has no margin for error. Paterson Water Heater provides the experience and planning these systems demand, from recirculation pumps and mixing valves to multi-unit heater banks.

For a large facility, downtime is measured in lost revenue and tenant complaints, so we plan for prevention first. That means comprehensive system assessments, sizing matched to real occupancy, multiple heaters piped in parallel so a single failure never cuts off hot water, recirculation service, and scheduled flushing and descaling to hold capacity. If a unit does go down, our water heater repair technicians isolate it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect venting and draft, confirm adequate combustion air, test relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report with priority levels and specific recommended actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. We keep a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test dates, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Heavy-use equipment, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, is put on a more frequent service schedule.

Emergency Service

After-Hours Commercial Water Heater Response

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Paterson Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Paterson and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Our technicians are equipped to handle the full range of commercial water heater emergencies, including failed gas valves, burned-out elements, flame sensor and ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, recirculation pumps that stopped, and tankless error-code lockouts. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, isolating a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Maintenance Programs

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your property's specific equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include tank flushing and anode checks, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature readings at the tap. Hard water shortens the life of commercial equipment fast, so high-use properties often need quarterly visits.

Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties that stay on a schedule typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower fuel bills. Call us to discuss a plan sized to your property and budget.
